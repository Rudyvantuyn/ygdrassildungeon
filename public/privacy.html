Privacy and terms

Ygdrassil · last updated 16 November 2026 · back to Ygdrassil

In short

You can play Ygdrassil without an account; your game is then saved only in your own browser. If you create an account, play together with others, or send us feedback, we store the little that is needed for that, in the EU, and nothing more. We do not sell data, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

Who is responsible

Ygdrassil is made by Rudy van Tuijn (the Netherlands), who is responsible for the data described here. For any question or request about your data, use Feedback → Something else in the game (System or Pack), and mention how to reach you.

What we store, and why

WhatWhyWhere, how long
Your saved game and settings So you can continue where you left off. Only in your browser's storage, until you delete it. We never see it.
Account: e-mail address, password (stored scrambled, never readable), player name. Signing in with Google gives us your e-mail address and your name from Google (we show only your first name). A guest account holds no e-mail at all, only a random id, until you link it To sign in, play together and keep a cloud save. Only if you create an account. Supabase (Frankfurt, EU), until you ask us to delete your account.
Cloud save So you can carry on in another browser or device, and see your party on the website. Kept automatically while you are signed in, at most every few minutes. Supabase (EU), until you overwrite it or delete your account.
Expeditions: your player name, the adventurer you bring, and what happens on the expedition; for a private expedition, a scrambled (hashed) copy of its password To run a shared expedition fairly (the server keeps the rules). Supabase (EU). Other players in the expedition see your player name and adventurer; while a gathering is open to all, anyone looking for one sees its host's player name. Deleted 90 days after the expedition's last move.
Runestones you carve: your player name, the floor and square, and the message (built from fixed words only), with how often other players heeded it or marked it false and who did (so everyone judges a stone once); and how much Heed gold you received today (a daily cap) So other players find your stone in their own game, and you hear when it helped someone. Supabase (EU). Every signed-in player can read the stone and your player name. A stone is deleted when it crumbles (your 21st stone, enough "false" marks) and at the latest 90 days after it was carved or last heeded.
Purchases and gifts: which cosmetic items your account owns, and for each payment its Stripe reference, the amount, what it was for and your account id (if you were signed in). Card and bank details go only to Stripe, never to us To give you what you bought, on every device you sign in on, and to keep the records the law requires for sales. Supabase (EU) for the items you own, as long as your account exists. Payment records are kept for 7 years (Dutch tax law). Stripe processes the payment under its own privacy policy.
Progress counts: when you reach a first step in the game (start a new game, found your party, first descent, first won fight, the first guardians, the second chapter, being carried home), a counter for that step and that day goes up by one. Your browser remembers that it counted, so each step counts once. Nothing else is sent: no account, name, save or address So the makers can see where new players stop, and make that part better. Supabase (EU), as one number per step per day. It cannot be traced back to you, so there is nothing to delete.
Book of Deeds: which deeds you earned, when, and the name of the adventurer who led your party then So your deeds are kept safe and follow you to other devices. Only when you are signed in. Supabase (EU). Only you can read them. Deleted with your account.
Shades: the floor, square and monster where a whole party fell, linked to your account only to limit how often one can be left So other players see where parties fell. Supabase (EU). Other players see the shade, never who left it. Deleted after 30 days.
Feedback and crash reports: your message, where you were in the game (screen, floor and square, your party's classes and levels, number of battles, time played, whether on an expedition), screen size, game version, and a name or contact only if you fill one in. Crash reports also hold the error details and your browser type. No account id is sent To find and fix problems and to improve the game. Crash reports are sent automatically when the game breaks. Supabase (EU). Only the maker can read them; they are deleted once handled, and at the latest after 12 months.
The Wayfinders' Stone: a name (and a short line about what you did), only for players who left a name with their feedback and whom the maker chose to thank To thank playtesters in the game. Supabase (EU). Every player can read the stone. Ask through Feedback to have your name taken off, and it is removed.
Visitor statistics: which page was opened, the site you came from, country, device type, operating system and browser. No cookies; visitors are counted with a code that changes every day, so you cannot be followed over time To see how many people play and where they found the game. Vercel Web Analytics, only as totals. No personal profile is made.
Technical logs (such as IP address) kept by our hosting Needed to deliver the website and keep it secure. Vercel (website) and Supabase, for a short period under their own policies.

The legal basis is the agreement to provide the game to you (accounts, cloud saves, expeditions) and our legitimate interest in a working, secure game (feedback, crash reports, logs). Fonts and art are served with the game itself, so no third-party font service sees your visit; the only visitor counting is the cookieless Vercel Web Analytics described above.

Your rights

You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). Ygdrassil is not meant for children under 13; parents can ask us to remove a child's account.
