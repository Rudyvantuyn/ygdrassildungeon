Photographs

The places in the game are real photographs, turned into pixel art by the game's own filter (cropped, reduced to the game's colour palette, and in some cases with modern details painted out). Thank you to these photographers for sharing their work.

Monsters

The monsters are pixel art from the Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup tiles (as are the chests, springs, gathering spots and gates in the corridors), drawn by the artists of the open-source game Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup and released under CC0. Thank you to everyone who drew them.

Icons

Map and marker icons from game-icons.net, by Lorc and Delapouite, licensed under CC BY 3.0. Recoloured and set in medallions.
