Privacy and terms

Ygdrassil · last updated 30 October 2026 · back to the game

In short

You can play Ygdrassil without an account; your game is then saved only in your own browser. If you create an account, play together with others, or send us feedback, we store the little that is needed for that, in the EU, and nothing more. We do not sell data, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

Who is responsible

Ygdrassil is made by Rudy van Tuijn (the Netherlands), who is responsible for the data described here. For any question or request about your data, use Feedback → Something else in the game (System or Pack), and mention how to reach you.

What we store, and why

WhatWhyWhere, how long
Your saved game and settings So you can continue where you left off. Only in your browser's storage, until you delete it. We never see it.
Account: e-mail address, password (stored scrambled, never readable), player name To sign in, play together and keep a cloud save. Only if you create an account. Supabase (Frankfurt, EU), until you ask us to delete your account.
Cloud save Only when you choose to save to the cloud. Supabase (EU), until you overwrite it or delete your account.
Expeditions: your player name, the adventurer you bring, and what happens on the expedition To run a shared expedition fairly (the server keeps the rules). Supabase (EU). Other players in the expedition see your player name and adventurer.
Feedback and crash reports: your message, where you were in the game (floor, levels, settings), screen size, browser type, game version, and your account id if you are signed in To find and fix problems and to improve the game. Crash reports are sent automatically when the game breaks. Supabase (EU). Only the maker can read them; they are deleted once handled, and at the latest after 12 months.
Technical logs (such as IP address) kept by our hosting Needed to deliver the website and keep it secure. Vercel (website) and Supabase, for a short period under their own policies.

The legal basis is the agreement to provide the game to you (accounts, cloud saves, expeditions) and our legitimate interest in a working, secure game (feedback, crash reports, logs). Fonts and art are served with the game itself; no third-party font or analytics service sees your visit.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your data, or to receive a copy of it, and you may object to its use. We answer within a month. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). Ygdrassil is not meant for children under 13; parents can ask us to remove a child's account.
